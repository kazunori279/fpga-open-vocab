# Superseded: "tight close-up" in the edit prompt, book open against closed

The second attempt, kept as the evidence for the fault that cropping the source
avoids. Asking the generator for a close-up closes the framing gap and grants
it licence to re-compose: two blind judges agreed the scene had survived on
only 7 and 8 of 12 pairs, and on the worst of them one side is a different
room, or the book has been replaced by a green bin.

Both judges named the open side correctly on 11 of 12 and agreed with each
other on 12 of 12, so the screen itself was sound — see
[`judge-a.json`](judge-a.json) and [`judge-b.json`](judge-b.json). Screening
this set down to its five clean pairs left the student at 3 of 5, which is
where the suspicion started that filtering was not going to be the answer.
