  lost   = probe_ceiling.py's column: its oracle minus the rule, in points.
  SWAP   = of those points, the ones lost to the enrolment choosing the direction
           the held-out frames disagree with. Not a misplaced cut - a backwards one.
  cut    = the rest: the best cut in the rule's own direction, minus the rule.
           lost = SWAP + cut, exactly.
  =state = scoring the held-out frames against the single threshold t_rule
           reproduces the rule's own accuracy. If this is not `ok` everywhere,
           the reduction is wrong and the rest of the table means nothing.
  ENROL  = (t_rule - t_mid)/s, the references not being where the frames are.
  ASYM   = (t_mid - t_best)/s, the price of cutting at the midpoint of the means.
           ENROL + ASYM is the whole distance from the rule's cut to the best one
           in its own direction, and it is what `cut` is made of.
  dA, dB = each reference's own displacement, signed so positive costs accuracy.
  drift  = the margin axis's own slope over the run, in s per 100 frames, after
           each class's mean is removed - so what is left is shared by both classes
           and is the run rather than the objects.
  s      = pooled within-class SD of the held-out margins, the unit for all of them.
