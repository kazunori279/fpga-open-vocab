Two independent runs differ with SD 8.3*sqrt(2) = 11.7 points, so a gap of
about 23 points is what 95% significance costs. With five runs the SD estimate
itself is loose - four degrees of freedom - so read 23 as "roughly twenty" and
not as a threshold.
